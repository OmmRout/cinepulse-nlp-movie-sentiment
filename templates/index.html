Implemented Security Protocols

  • Strict Content Security Policy (CSP): Self-hosted scripts & Chart.js bundle (script-src 'self'), blocked framing (frame-ancestors 'none'), and object blocking.
  • CSRF & Same-Origin Guard: Session-bound cryptographic tokens verified via constant-time hmac.compare_digest on every POST endpoint.
  • Rate Limiting & Payload Caps: Sliding-window limits via Flask-Limiter (60/min predict, 12/min batch), 10,000-char input cap, 2 MB / 500-row upload cap.
  • XSS & CSV Formula Injection Defense: Null-byte & control-char rejection, safe DOM textContent rendering, and OWASP cell-prefix neutralization on CSV export.
  • Transport & Cookie Hardening: HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, X-Frame-Options: DENY, HttpOnly & SameSite=Lax cookies.
